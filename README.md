Blast Assist is a mobile application that helps mining personnel perform blasting calculations quickly and accurately. Users enter blasting information such as rock conditions and blast area, and the app automatically calculates the required explosive type, hole depth, spacing, and other blasting parameters. The app stores all blast records for future reference, allows users to view and mark blast locations on a map, and sends safety notifications to nearby community members before scheduled blasting activities. It can also work offline and synchronize data when an internet connection becomes available.
